# Trance — Genre Skill

## Tempo
- Range: 135-150 BPM
- Sweet spot: 138-140 BPM

## Key Preferences
- Minor keys: Am, Dm, Fm — emotional, euphoric
- Harmonic minor for exotic tension

## Iconic Chord Progressions
1. vi-IV-I-V (euphoric anthem, ~35%)
2. i-bVI-bVII-i (epic minor, ~30%)
3. i-bIII-bVI-bVII (ascending minor, ~20%)

## Drum Pattern Family
- Four-on-the-floor kick
- Off-beat hi-hat / open hat on up-beats
- Clap on 2 and 4
- Hi-hat rolls in buildups (32nd notes)

## Instrumentation
- Core: supersaw lead, pad, sub bass, kick
- Common: pluck/arp synth, vocal chop, riser FX
- Avoid: acoustic/organic instruments

## Section Structure
- Intro: 8-16 bars
- Buildup 1: 8-16 bars (rising tension)
- Drop 1: 16-32 bars (full energy)
- Breakdown: 8-16 bars (stripped, emotional)
- Buildup 2: 8-16 bars
- Drop 2: 16-32 bars
- Outro: 8-16 bars

## Cliches to AVOID
- Breakdowns without emotional arc
- Drops that don't deliver on buildup tension
- Overly busy melodies that clash with the arpeggiated texture
