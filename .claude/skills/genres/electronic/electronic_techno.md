# Techno — Genre Skill

## Tempo
- Range: 128-140 BPM
- Sweet spot: 130-135 BPM
- Sub-genres extend to 145+ (hard techno) or 120 (deep techno)

## Key Preferences
- Minor keys dominate: Am, Dm, Em
- Often modal or atonal — harmony is secondary to rhythm and timbre
- Phrygian mode for dark, industrial textures

## Iconic Chord Progressions
1. Single chord vamp (drone/pedal) (~40%)
2. i-bVII oscillation (~25%)
3. i-iv two-chord vamp (~20%)
4. Chromatic bass motion i-bII-i (~15%)

## Drum Pattern Family
- Four-on-the-floor kick at every quarter note
- Closed hi-hat on off-beats or sixteenth notes
- Clap/snare on beats 2 and 4
- Percussion layers: shakers, rides, metallic hits

## Instrumentation
- Core: kick drum, hi-hat, synth bass, synth lead/stab
- Common: pad/drone, clap, percussion layers
- Avoid: acoustic instruments, melodic complexity, vocal

## Section Structure
- Intro: 8-16 bars, gradual element introduction
- Build: 8 bars, rising filter/intensity
- Peak: 16-32 bars, full groove
- Breakdown: 4-8 bars, stripped elements
- Peak 2: 16-32 bars, variation on first peak
- Outro: 8-16 bars, gradual removal

## Cliches to AVOID
- Melodic content that distracts from the rhythmic hypnosis
- Too many harmonic changes — techno is about repetition and variation in timbre
- Breakdowns that fully stop momentum
