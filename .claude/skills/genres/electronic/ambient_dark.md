# Dark Ambient — Genre Skill

## Tempo
- Range: 40-80 BPM (or beatless)
- Often no perceivable tempo — time is measured in evolution

## Key Preferences
- Minor keys: Am, Cm, Dm
- Chromatic and microtonal elements for unease
- Phrygian for semi-tone root movement tension

## Iconic Chord Progressions
1. Single chord drone / pedal point (~50%)
2. I-bII semitone oscillation (~25%)
3. Slow chromatic descent (~15%)
4. Modal ambiguity — no clear tonal center (~10%)

## Drum Pattern Family
- Typically beatless — no standard drum pattern
- Occasional sub-bass pulses or textural percussion
- When percussion exists: sparse, reverb-heavy hits

## Instrumentation
- Core: synth pads, drones, processed field recordings
- Common: bowed strings (cello), processed piano, sub bass
- Avoid: drums, bright instruments, anything rhythmically driving

## Section Structure
- Not traditional sections — more like phases of evolution
- Phase 1: 8-16 bars (establish texture/drone)
- Phase 2: 16-32 bars (introduce elements, slow transformation)
- Transition: 4-8 bars (tension shift)
- Phase 3: 16-32 bars (peak density or darkest moment)
- Fade: 8-16 bars (dissolution)

## Cliches to AVOID
- Jump scares or sudden loud events — dark ambient is sustained tension
- Too much activity — the power is in what you don't hear
- Overly melodic content that breaks the immersive texture
