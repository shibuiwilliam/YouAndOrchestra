# Neo Soul — Genre Skill

## Tempo
- Range: 70-100 BPM
- Sweet spot: 80-90 BPM

## Key Preferences
- Flat keys: Eb, Ab, Bb, Db (warm on Rhodes/Wurlitzer)
- Major or dorian for warmth, mixolydian for bluesy edge

## Iconic Chord Progressions
1. ii9-V9-Imaj9 (jazz-influenced, ~30%)
2. Imaj9-IVmaj9-iii7-vi9 (~25%)
3. iv9-bVII9-Imaj9 (modal interchange, ~20%)
4. II9-V9-Imaj9 (secondary dominant, ~15%)

## Drum Pattern Family
- Laid-back groove with ghost notes on snare
- Kick: syncopated, locking with bass
- Hi-hat: sixteenth notes with dynamics
- Ghost notes essential for feel

## Instrumentation
- Core: electric piano (Rhodes/Wurlitzer), bass guitar, drums
- Common: guitar (clean/muted), synth pad, horn section
- Avoid: distorted guitars, heavy percussion

## Section Structure
- Intro: 4 bars (instrumental groove establishing)
- Verse: 8 bars (understated, groove-forward)
- Pre-chorus: 4 bars (harmonic build)
- Chorus: 8 bars (melodic peak, richer chords)
- Bridge: 4-8 bars (harmonic departure)

## Cliches to AVOID
- Stiff, quantized drums without ghost notes
- Too many chord changes — the groove needs room to breathe
- Ignoring dynamics within phrases
