# Heavy Metal — Genre Skill

## Tempo
- Range: 120-200 BPM
- Sweet spot: 140-160 BPM (driving energy)
- Doom/sludge: 60-80 BPM; thrash/speed: 180-220 BPM

## Key Preferences
- Guitar-friendly: E, D, C (drop tunings), A, B
- Minor keys, phrygian mode for dark/aggressive character
- Harmonic minor for neoclassical flavor

## Iconic Chord Progressions
1. i-bVII-bVI-bVII (aeolian descent, ~30%)
2. i-bII-i (tritone/semitone tension, ~25%)
3. i-iv-v-i (natural minor, ~25%)
4. Chromatic riff-based (non-functional, ~20%)

## Drum Pattern Family
- Double kick: constant or alternating sixteenths
- Snare on 2 and 4 (or blast beat: every beat)
- China/crash accents at riff changes
- Fills: tom rolls, double-kick flurries

## Instrumentation
- Core: distorted electric guitar(s), bass guitar, drum kit
- Common: second rhythm guitar, lead guitar
- Avoid: acoustic instruments, delicate timbres

## Section Structure
- Intro: 4-8 bars (riff statement or atmospheric buildup)
- Verse: 8 bars (palm-muted riff, moderate intensity)
- Pre-chorus: 4 bars (building tension)
- Chorus: 8 bars (full power, open chords)
- Solo: 8-16 bars (virtuosic lead over verse/chorus)
- Breakdown: 4-8 bars (half-time, heaviest section)

## Cliches to AVOID
- Monotonous palm-muted chugging without melodic shape
- Blast beats sustained too long without dynamic relief
- Solos disconnected from the song's harmonic content
