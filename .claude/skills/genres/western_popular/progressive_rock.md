# Progressive Rock — Genre Skill

## Tempo
- Range: 80-160 BPM (often variable within a piece)
- Slow passages: 60-80 BPM (atmospheric/lyrical)
- Fast passages: 140-180 BPM (virtuosic/climactic)

## Key Preferences
- Minor keys: Em, Am, Dm (dramatic intensity)
- Major keys: C, G (pastoral sections)
- Lydian mode for floating, ethereal quality
- Frequent modulations between sections

## Iconic Chord Progressions
1. I-bVII-IV-I (mixolydian rock, ~25%)
2. I-bVI-bVII-I (modal rock, ~25%)
3. ii-V-I-bVII (jazz-influenced, ~20%)
4. Through-composed (non-repeating, ~30%)

## Drum Pattern Family
- Standard rock backbeat as foundation
- Odd meters: 5/4, 7/8, 7/4, 11/8
- Mixed meter: alternating bars of different time signatures
- Polyrhythmic patterns between instruments

## Instrumentation
- Core: electric guitar, bass guitar, drums, keyboard
- Signature: mellotron, synthesizer, organ
- Common: flute, violin, acoustic guitar (contrast sections)
- Avoid: drum machines, auto-tuned vocals

## Section Structure
- Not verse-chorus — through-composed or suite-like
- Exposition: 8-16 bars (theme statement)
- Development: 16-32 bars (harmonic/metric exploration)
- Contrast section: 8-16 bars (different tempo/meter/mood)
- Recapitulation: 8-16 bars (return of themes, possibly transformed)
- Coda: 4-8 bars

## Cliches to AVOID
- Odd meters used as a showcase rather than serving the music
- Extended solos that lose connection to the piece's themes
- Complexity at the expense of emotional impact
