# Traditional Country — Genre Skill

## Tempo
- Range: 100-140 BPM
- Sweet spot: 110-120 BPM
- Ballads: 70-95 BPM

## Key Preferences
- Guitar-friendly major keys: G, D, C, A, E
- Mixolydian mode for bluesy inflections (bVII)

## Iconic Chord Progressions
1. I-IV-V-I (country standard, ~40%)
2. I-V-vi-IV (~25%)
3. I-IV-I-V (two-chord verse, ~20%)

## Drum Pattern Family
- Train beat: snare pattern mimicking locomotive
- Boom-chick: kick on 1&3, snare on 2&4, with shuffle
- Brushes for ballads

## Instrumentation
- Core: acoustic guitar, bass, drums
- Signature: pedal steel, fiddle
- Common: piano, banjo, dobro
- Avoid: synths, distorted guitars, electronic drums

## Section Structure
- Intro: 4 bars (guitar or fiddle lick)
- Verse: 8 bars (storytelling, moderate dynamics)
- Chorus: 8 bars (melodic hook, fuller arrangement)
- Bridge: 4 bars (often instrumental — fiddle or steel solo)
- Outro: 4 bars (tag ending or fade)

## Cliches to AVOID
- Generic I-IV-V with no melodic variation
- Pedal steel as decoration rather than a melodic voice
- Overly polished production that eliminates acoustic character
