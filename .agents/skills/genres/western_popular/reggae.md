# Roots Reggae — Genre Skill

## Tempo
- Range: 70-90 BPM
- Sweet spot: 75-82 BPM

## Key Preferences
- Major keys: G, C, D (bright, optimistic)
- Minor keys: Am, Em (roots, conscious lyrics)
- Mixolydian for a bluesy warmth

## Iconic Chord Progressions
1. I-IV-V-I (roots standard, ~35%)
2. I-V-vi-IV (~25%)
3. i-bVII-bVI-bVII (minor roots, ~20%)

## Drum Pattern Family
- One-drop: kick on beat 3 only (defining feature)
- Snare/rim on beat 3 with kick
- Hi-hat: steady eighth notes
- Steppers variant: kick on every beat

## Instrumentation
- Core: bass guitar (heavy, melodic), guitar (skank), drums, organ
- Common: horns (trumpet, trombone), percussion
- Avoid: distorted guitar, heavy synths

## Section Structure
- Intro: 4 bars (bass + drums establishing groove)
- Verse: 8 bars (vocal-driven, moderate dynamics)
- Chorus: 8 bars (melodic hook, full band)
- Bridge: 4 bars (instrumental — organ or horn solo)
- Dub break: 4-8 bars (stripped, delay effects)

## Cliches to AVOID
- Monotonous skank guitar without any dynamic shape
- Bass lines that are too busy — the pocket is king
- Losing the one-drop feel by adding too many kick hits
