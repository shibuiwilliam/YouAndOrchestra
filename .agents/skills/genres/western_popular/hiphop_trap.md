# Trap — Genre Skill

## Tempo
- Range: 130-170 BPM (felt as half-time: 65-85 BPM)
- Sweet spot: 140-150 BPM

## Key Preferences
- Dark minor keys: C#m, Cm, Gm
- Phrygian and harmonic minor for tension

## Iconic Chord Progressions
1. i-bVI-bVII-i (dark minor loop, ~35%)
2. i-iv-bVI-bVII (~25%)
3. i-bII-i (semitone tension, ~20%)
4. Single chord drone (~20%)

## Drum Pattern Family
- Half-time snare (beat 3 only in the felt half-time)
- 808 kick with pitch slides
- Hi-hat: rapid rolls (32nd notes), open hats on off-beats
- Sparse arrangement — space is a feature

## Instrumentation
- Core: 808 bass, drum machine, dark pad/lead
- Common: vocal samples, bells, flute-type leads
- Avoid: organic/acoustic instruments

## Section Structure
- Intro: 4-8 bars
- Verse: 8-16 bars (sparse, rhythmic)
- Hook: 4-8 bars (melodic peak)
- Drop: 8 bars (bass-heavy)
- Outro: 4-8 bars

## Cliches to AVOID
- Hi-hat patterns with no variation or dynamics
- 808 bass so heavy it eliminates all other sonic detail
- Identical section structures throughout
